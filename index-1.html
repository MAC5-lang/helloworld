For You, Bitso

I kept my words inside my chest,
afraid to let them show,
but every quiet thought I had
was yours, and let you know.

If time could slow its hurried hands,
I’d stay a little more,
just to be near your gentle smile,
the one I’m looking for.

So here’s my heart on paper now,
no silence, no more fear.
Whatever you decide to say,
I’m glad that you are here. ❤️
